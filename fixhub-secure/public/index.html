Privacy & data safety

How ServePoint protects you

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work: your Google name and email when you sign in, the booking details you type in, your area — detected automatically (precise GPS only with your permission, otherwise an approximate area from your network) — for dispatch, and your trade and service area if you join as an artisan.

What stays on your phone

Your exact street address, voice notes and full job photos never leave this device. Only a small "photo attached" flag syncs so the artisan knows to expect one.

How it is stored

Bookings live in a secured Supabase database with row-level security: you can only read your own bookings, artisans only see open requests, and customers only see applications on their own requests. Nobody can delete records through the app.

Payments

Card and Mobile Money payments open a Paystack secure popup. ServePoint never sees or stores your MoMo PIN or card number. Money is held in escrow and released to the artisan only after you confirm the work.

Location & tracking

Live trip progress — map position, arrival time and detour alerts — is shared between you and your artisan or customer only while a job is active.

Your choices

Use ServePoint as a guest without signing in. Log out any time in Account to clear your session on this phone. To ask for a copy or deletion of your data, call support on 0800 349 548.
